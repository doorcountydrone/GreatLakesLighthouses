Great Lakes Lighthouses — Pico 2 W wiring
============================================

Power
-----
- Pico 2 W: USB or 5 V on VSYS (with a diode / proper supply).
- WS2812 / NeoPixel strip: 5 V sized for LED count. 13 LEDs is easy from USB;
  a long map strip needs its own 5 V supply.
- Common GND between Pico and LED strip is required.

Default pins (same layout as MetarMap)
--------------------------------------
  Function              GPIO
  Strip data            0
  LED matrix (optional) 1  (8x32 WS2812, 5 V + common GND; not from GPIO 18)
  Force setup AP        15  (momentary button to GND; hold ~3 s at boot)
  LDR read (optional)  26  (ADC0)
  OLED 128x64 I2C (optional)
    SDA / data          16
    SCL / clock         17
    3.3 V from Pico     18  (firmware sets this pin HIGH)
    GND from Pico       19  (firmware sets this pin LOW)

  Keep the 10k. In a bright room the LDR resistance drops near a short; the
  10k is what limits current from 3.3 V into GPIO 26. 47k also works but sits
  in a narrower ADC range.

  3.3 V -- LDR -- GPIO 26 -- 10k -- GND

  This LDR reads a low ADC in bright light (~700) and higher when covered
  (~16000). The strip follows that: bright room dims toward min, dark uses
  max. Raise min brightness if you still want a glow in daylight. Do not
  wire the LDR from 3.3 V straight to GND. GPIO 21 is free.

  OLED: copy ssd1306.py next to main.py. The screen rotates IP / LDR / METAR.
  LED matrix: copy led_matrix.py next to main.py. 8x32 on GPIO 1, snake-column
  wiring (same as MetarMap). Scrolls IP, METAR, and light names. Power the
  matrix from 5 V, not GPIO 18. If letters look mirrored, change WIRING in
  led_matrix.py to COLUMN_MAJOR, SNAKE_ROW, or ROW_MAJOR.

LED order (south to north)
-------------------------
  0  Kewaunee Pierhead
  1  Algoma Pierhead
  2  Sturgeon Bay Ship Canal Light
  3  Sturgeon Bay North Pierhead
  4  Sherwood Point
  5  Eagle Bluff
  6  Chambers Island
  7  Baileys Harbor Range Lights
  8  Old Baileys Harbor (Birdcage)
  9  Cana Island
 10  Plum Island Range Lights
 11  Pilot Island
 12  Pottawatomie (Rock Island)

If your physical strip is longer, extra LEDs stay off. Set num_leds in setup
to the number of WS2812 chips on the wire.

Colors and flash (real characteristics)
---------------------------------------
  White  W   Canal-style lantern white
  Red    R   navigation red (pierhead, canal, ranges)

  Fl     flashing (short on, longer off)
  Iso    isophase (equal on and off)
  F      fixed (steady)
  Fl(2)  group of two flashes in one period

See README.md for each LED's official characteristic.
