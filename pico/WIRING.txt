Great Lakes Lighthouses — Pico 2 W wiring
============================================

Power
-----
- Pico 2 W: USB or 5 V on VSYS (with a diode / proper supply).
- WS2812 / NeoPixel strip: 5 V sized for LED count. 13 LEDs is easy from USB;
  a long map strip needs its own 5 V supply.
- Common GND between Pico and LED strip is required.

Default pins (same layout as MetarMap)
--------------------------------------
  Function              GPIO
  Strip data            0
  Force setup AP        15  (momentary button to GND; hold ~3 s at boot)
  LDR drive (optional)  21  (output high)
  LDR read (optional)   26  (ADC0); 10 kΩ to GND on the ADC side

LED order (south → north)
-------------------------
  0  Kewaunee Pierhead
  1  Algoma Pierhead
  2  Sturgeon Bay Ship Canal Light
  3  Sturgeon Bay North Pierhead
  4  Sherwood Point
  5  Eagle Bluff
  6  Chambers Island
  7  Baileys Harbor Range Lights
  8  Old Baileys Harbor (Birdcage)
  9  Cana Island
 10  Plum Island Range Lights
 11  Pilot Island
 12  Pottawatomie (Rock Island)

If your physical strip is longer, extra LEDs stay off. Set num_leds in setup
to the number of WS2812 chips on the wire.

Colors and flash (real characteristics)
---------------------------------------
  White  W   Canal-style lantern white
  Red    R   navigation red (pierhead, canal, ranges)

  Fl     flashing (short on, longer off)
  Iso    isophase (equal on and off)
  F      fixed (steady)
  Fl(2)  group of two flashes in one period

See README.md for each LED's official characteristic.
